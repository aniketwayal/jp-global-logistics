Dangerous Goods (DG) Handling

AryaGlobe Logistics Pvt. Ltd. provides safe, compliant and reliable Dangerous Goods (DG) Logistics Services for both hazardous and non-hazardous chemicals worldwide. Our qualified DG specialists ensure every shipment is packed, documented, labeled and transported in accordance with IATA, IMDG and applicable international regulations, ensuring maximum safety and regulatory compliance.

We manage every stage of the DG logistics process with strict adherence to international safety standards, offering secure transportation solutions for a wide range of dangerous goods across air, ocean and multimodal freight networks.

Certified Handling • Safe Transportation • Global DG Solutions

OUR SERVICES INCLUDE PACKING & TRANSPORTATION OF DANGEROUS GOODS AS PER IATA REGULATION.

Our certified Dangerous Goods specialists handle a broad range of hazardous cargo while ensuring complete compliance with international transportation regulations.

  • Class 2 – Gases
  • Class 3 – Flammable Liquids
  • Class 4 – Flammable Solids
  • Class 5 – Oxidizing Substances & Organic Peroxides
  • Class 6 – Toxic & Infectious Substances
  • Class 7 – Radioactive Materials
  • Class 8 – Corrosive Substances
  • Class 9 – Miscellaneous Dangerous Goods
