Dangerous Goods (DG) Handling

AryanGlob Logistics Pvt. Ltd. provides safe, compliant, and reliable Dangerous Goods (DG) Logistics Services for both hazardous and non-hazardous chemicals worldwide. Our qualified DG specialists ensure that every shipment is packed, documented, labeled, and transported in accordance with IATA, IMDG, and applicable international regulations, ensuring maximum safety and regulatory compliance.

We Handle:

  • Class 2 – Gases
  • Class 3 – Flammable Liquids
  • Class 4 – Flammable Solids
  • Class 5 – Oxidizing Substances & Organic Peroxides
  • Class 6 – Toxic & Infectious Substances
  • Class 7 – Radioactive Materials
  • Class 8 – Corrosive Substances
  • Class 9 – Miscellaneous Dangerous Goods
