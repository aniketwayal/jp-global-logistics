Project Cargo

AryaGlobe Logistics Pvt. Ltd. specializes in Project Cargo, Heavy Lift, Over-Dimensional (ODC), and Out-of-Gauge (OOG) shipments, providing complete logistics solutions for complex and oversized cargo movements. Our experienced team ensures detailed planning, precise coordination, and efficient execution to deliver every project safely, on time, and within budget.

We offer customized turnkey logistics solutions, from order planning and documentation to transportation, customs clearance, and final delivery. With extensive knowledge of international routes and a strong global network, we handle heavy machinery, industrial equipment, rolling stock, and other large indivisible cargo with the highest level of professionalism.
