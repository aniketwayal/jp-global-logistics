Ocean Freight Services

AryaGlobe Logistics provides dependable and cost-effective Ocean Freight solutions for both import and export shipments worldwide. Our experienced logistics professionals work closely with major international shipping lines to ensure reliable, secure and timely cargo movement.

We specialize in Full Container Load (FCL), Less than Container Load (LCL), Break Bulk Cargo, Project Cargo and Out-of-Gauge (OOG) transportation. Whether your shipment is small or oversized, we provide customized logistics solutions to meet your business requirements.

Our comprehensive services include booking, documentation, customs clearance, container handling, cargo tracking and final delivery, ensuring a seamless shipping experience from origin to destination.

Reasons for Choosing Ocean Freight

Ocean Freight is the most economical and dependable solution for transporting large volumes of cargo across international markets. AryaGlobe Logistics offers customized sea freight services that ensure safe handling, competitive pricing and reliable transit schedules.

01

Full Container Load (FCL)

Dedicated container shipping provides maximum security, faster handling and complete control over your cargo. Ideal for high-volume international shipments requiring reliable transit schedules.

02

Less than Container Load (LCL)

Share container space and pay only for the volume you use. LCL is an economical option for small and medium shipments without compromising safety or reliability.

03

Cargo Insurance & Safety

Comprehensive cargo protection minimizes transportation risks and provides financial security against unforeseen events during international transit.

04

Warehousing & Distribution

Integrated warehousing, inventory management and nationwide distribution services simplify your supply chain and improve delivery efficiency.

05

Global Multi-Modal Logistics

Combine Ocean Freight with road, rail and air transportation for optimized delivery schedules, competitive freight costs and complete end-to-end logistics solutions.

06

Worldwide Port Connectivity

Through our global network of shipping partners, we provide seamless import and export services connecting major ports across Asia, Europe, the Middle East, Africa and the Americas.
