Overview

AryanGlob Logistics Pvt. Ltd. provides reliable and cost-effective Ocean Freight Services for both Import and Export shipments. We offer FCL (Full Container Load), LCL (Less than Container Load), Break Bulk, Project Cargo, and Out-of-Gauge (OOG) Cargo solutions with complete door-to-door logistics.

Working with all major shipping lines operating to and from India, we ensure competitive freight rates, secure cargo handling, and timely deliveries. From order placement to final delivery, our experienced team maintains complete control over every stage of your shipment.

Our ocean freight expertise includes handling large-volume shipments to and from Europe, the USA, the Far East, the Middle East, Africa, and other global destinations. We specialize in planning and coordinating oversized and project cargo, delivering economical and efficient transportation while meeting strict delivery schedules.
